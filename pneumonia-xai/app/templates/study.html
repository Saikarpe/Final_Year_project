Study mode

Randomised, counterbalanced case presentation for the human-evaluation study (E-12–E-16).

Not available

This screen is intentionally not implemented against fabricated data. Two real prerequisites are missing, and neither is something this codebase can produce on its own:

  • P-1, IEC ethics approval — protocol, participant information sheet, consent form, and data-handling statement, reviewed by an institutional ethics committee.
  • D-6, a frozen study case set — 40–60 stratified cases (including the ~25% deliberately-wrong subset) drawn from a source with radiologist-verified ground truth, which in turn depends on D-2 (VinDr-CXR, credentialed access pending).

When D-6 and P-1 land, this becomes a UI task, not a data-modelling one: xai_cxr.auditlog already shows the pattern (one append-only SQLite table) that a study_responses table would follow. See docs/decisions_log.md.
